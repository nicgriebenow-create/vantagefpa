Built for sponsors and independent buyers underwriting a target, for brokers and deal teams who need every figure to carry its source, and for founders getting ready to sell. The Method

Four checks, each independent of the others

Every bank account, every month, each identity proven to a tolerance of one dollar. A month that clears all four is reconciled. A month that fails any of them lands in a review register with the failing check quoted, never absorbed into a plug.

Check One
The statement self-foots

Opening plus inflows minus outflows must equal the closing balance, using only the statement's own figures.

Check Two
Closing ties to the ledger

The statement closing balance must equal the general ledger cash balance for the same month.

Check Three
The flow ties

The statement's net flow must equal the change in ledger cash from the prior month to this one.

Check Four
The opening ties back

The statement opening balance must equal the prior month's ledger cash closing balance.

With the Ledger Detail

The package goes deeper when the detail arrives

The trial balance is where the work starts, and the general ledger journal detail is where it deepens. These checks are built in and switch on when the detail arrives. Until it does, the workbook names them as pending rather than staying quiet.

Population, Not Sample
Cutoff Testing and GL Reconciliation

When the journal detail is supplied, it is footed to the trial balance account by account and period by period, and every break lands in a register rather than being absorbed. Cutoff testing runs over the whole population rather than a sample, once the detail carries both a transaction date and a posting date on the same line.

  • GL detail footed to the trial balance, account by account, when the journal detail is supplied
  • Cutoff tested over the full population once both date fields arrive
  • Every break registered on its own row, never absorbed
  • A pending block naming the missing field otherwise
Counterparties
Concentration and Related Parties

When the ledger detail carries counterparty names, the package builds revenue by customer with concentration, spend by vendor, and a related-party scan over those names. Where the names are not in the export, the tab says so and names the field that would close it.

  • Revenue by customer and concentration, when counterparty names are supplied
  • Spend by vendor from the same detail
  • Related-party scan across counterparty names
  • Customer concentration read the way a buyer reads it

Scope

What ships, and what does not

The fastest way to lose a deal room is to let a boundary get discovered at delivery. Scope is agreed in writing before the work starts, and the limits below are stated in every engagement letter rather than found later. Each draft also carries a table of the workstreams it did not perform, why, and what would close each one, on the tab a buyer opens first.

01
This Is Analytical Support, Not an Attest Engagement

Vantage FP&A LLC is not a CPA firm. The work is diligence and analytical support. It is not an audit, a review, a compilation, or an attest engagement, and it carries no opinion or assurance. Where a lender or an investment committee specifically requires a named CPA firm's engagement letter, Vantage says so on the first call rather than at delivery.

02
Judgment Items Ship Flagged Rather Than Filled

Some adjustments need a market input that a machine has no business inventing. Owner compensation is the clearest example. Those items are identified, evidenced, and handed to you unsized, with the basis stated in words so you can size them with your own comparables. The engine proves what it can prove and declines the rest out loud.

03
Proof of Cash Runs at the Account-Month Level

The reconciliation ties reported activity to bank statement activity for each account in each month. Line-level matching of bank activity against journal detail is the next layer of the build and is named as roadmap in writing before an engagement starts. Where a check depends on data that is not available, the workbook renders it as pending rather than approximating it.

04
No Valuation, No Price Opinion, No Tax or Legal Diligence

Vantage produces the evidence and the questions. What the findings mean for price, structure, or whether to proceed is yours and your advisors' call, and nothing in the package recommends a transaction. Tax and legal diligence sit with the specialists who own them.
