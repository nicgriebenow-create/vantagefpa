Monthly Reporting

Every month

We build the reporting package around the close you already run, starting from your accounting exports and the Excel files your team uses today. The board deck and the workbook come off the same numbers as the forecast, so they agree. Once it's built, we keep it running on a monthly retainer, with support through each close.

  • Board package. Deck and workbook built from the same numbers.
  • Variance analysis. Against budget and prior year, with EBITDA bridges.
  • Rest-of-year reforecast. A base case with downside and upside around it, refreshed each close.
  • KPIs. Computed from the same trial balance as the board package.
  • Peer view. Public companies in your SIC code, with a source link on each figure.

The Read Before the QoE

Before a deal

When a sale or an acquisition is coming, we read the books before the quality of earnings work starts. You get adjusted EBITDA with each add-back sourced, red flags with the evidence attached, cash tied to the bank when bank statements are supplied, and the questions a buyer's team is likely to ask. It is not an audit or a QoE, and it doesn't replace a QoE your lender requires.

  • Reported to adjusted EBITDA. Each add-back on its own row, with the account it came from and the evidence behind it.
  • Cash tied to the bank. By account and month, when bank statements are supplied.
  • Red flags. Each one with its evidence attached.
  • The likely questions. What a buyer's team will probably ask, each tied to the finding that raised it.
  • What the read didn't cover. Listed up front, with what would close each gap.

No language model in any numeric path
