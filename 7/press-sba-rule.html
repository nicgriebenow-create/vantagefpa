Vantage FP&A Opens a Pre-QoE Read as SBA 7(a) Quality of Earnings Rules Take Effect

AUSTIN, Texas, Oct. 1, 2026. Vantage FP&A today announced a pre-QoE read for transactions under the U.S. Small Business Administration's updated 7(a) procedures, SOP 50 10 8.1, effective today.

Under the procedure, SBA lenders financing certain acquisitions at or above a set purchase price threshold must obtain a quality of earnings report. In its words, "The QoE must include a Cash Proof. For purposes of this requirement, a Cash Proof is a financial analysis that reconstructs cash receipts and disbursements by reconciling bank statement data to the income statement and tax return for each period under review. The Cash Proof is designed to identify discrepancies in income and undisclosed expenses and must be performed on both a trailing 12-month basis and the last two fiscal years. If the acquired business has been operating for less than two years, the scope can be reduced accordingly." (SBA SOP 50 10 8.1, Appendix 15, 7(a) Changes of Ownership, Credit Standards, Financial Due Diligence.)

The required QoE is conducted for the lender's benefit and may not be prepared by or for the seller. With bank statements, Vantage reconciles the ledger's cash to the bank, by account and month. With income tax returns as filed, it reconciles book revenue, deductions, officer compensation and net income to each calendar-year return. Differences are listed with both figures, and we do not guess at the cause.

The work covers GL-to-bank and book-to-tax reconciliation. It is not a Cash Proof. It does not rebuild cash receipts and disbursements and reads no IRS transcripts. It does not cover every element the procedure lists. Each draft states which workstreams were not performed. It is not a quality of earnings report, an audit or assurance, and it does not replace the QoE the procedure requires the lender to obtain.

Rule trigger and Texas lending data

The change-of-ownership field is coded by the lender. An acquisition coded under another value is not counted.
